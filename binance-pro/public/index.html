Learn the basics

What is a spot trade?

You buy or sell the actual coin at the current price. Buy 1 ETH and you own 1 ETH.

What is a limit order?

An order that waits until the price reaches a level you choose, instead of trading right now.

What are futures?

Contracts that let you bet on a price going up (long) or down (short), often with leverage that multiplies gains and losses.

What is liquidation?

When losses on a leveraged position use up most of your margin, the position is closed automatically.

What is APY?

Annual percentage yield: the yearly return you earn on savings.

What is an order book?

A live list of buy orders (bids) and sell orders (asks). Trades happen where they meet.

What is a candlestick?

Each candle shows the open, high, low and close for a time period. Green means the price rose, red means it fell.

What is a trading fee?

A small percentage the exchange charges on each trade. Here it is 0.1%.

Staying safe

Never share passwords or seed phrases. Be careful with promises of guaranteed returns, and only trade money you can afford to lose.
